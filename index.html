I am a second-year Master's student in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Oliver Kroemer. I am interested in how robots can understand the physical world through interaction and exploration. I have also worked closely with Prof. Zeynep Temel on tactile manipulation.

Before CMU, I received my Bachelor's degree from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and Zhejiang University. During my undergraduate years, I had the fortune to work with Prof. Yunzhu Li at UIUC, and with Prof. Hua Chen and Prof. Liangjing Yang at Zhejiang University.
